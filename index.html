How to use Baseball Teams Explorer

  • Find a team: type in the search box, or scroll through the divisions.
  • See a roster: click any team card or standings row. Stats are for the current season.
  • Sort stats: click a column header in the roster (e.g. HR or ERA).
  • Player details: click a player for their bio and season line.
  • Favorites: tap ☆ to pin a team to "My Teams" at the top.
  • Colors: open ☰ Settings to pick a theme, including your favorite team's colors.
  • Keyboard: press / to search and Esc to close a window.

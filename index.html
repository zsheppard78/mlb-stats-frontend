    How to use Baseball Teams Explorer

    • Find a team: type in the search box, or scroll through the divisions.
    • See a roster: click any team in the standings. Stats are for the current season.
    • Sort stats: click a column header in the roster (e.g. HR or ERA).
    • Other seasons: use the Season menu next to search to see standings and rosters from any year back to 1969.
    • Player details: click a player for their bio, season line, career totals and year-by-year stats.
    • Compare players: open ☰ and choose Compare players, or press Compare in a player's window. Compare careers or a single season.
    • Favorites: tap ☆ next to a team to pin it to "My teams" at the top.
    • Colors: open ☰ Settings to pick a theme, including your favorite team's colors.
    • Keyboard: press / to search and Esc to close a window.

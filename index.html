      How to use MLB Stats Explorer

      • Find a team: type in the search box, or scroll through the divisions.
      • See a team: click any team in the standings for its roster, or switch to Games for recent results and what's coming up.
      • Other seasons: use the Season menu next to search to see standings and rosters from any year back to 1969.
      • Players: click a player for their bio, season, career, year-by-year chart, game log and splits.
      • From the ☰ menu: Today's games, League leaders, Find a player and Compare players.
      • Share: use Copy link in a team, player or comparison window to send exactly what you're looking at.
      • Favorites: tap ☆ next to a team to pin it to "My teams" at the top.
      • Colors: open ☰ and pick a theme, including your favorite team's colors.
      • Keyboard: press / to search teams and Esc to close a window.
